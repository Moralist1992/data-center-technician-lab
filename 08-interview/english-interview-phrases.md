# English Phrases for the Technical Interview

## Time / clarification
> Give me a second to think.

> Could you please repeat the question?

> Could you please explain the question in another way?

> Let me explain it another way.

## Uncertainty
> I'm not completely sure, so I would check the documentation or the runbook.

> I would first collect more information before taking action.

## Troubleshooting
> First, I would check the BMC.

> I would check the hardware event log.

> I would check the remote console.

> I would try to isolate the problem.

> I would follow the runbook.

> I would verify the result after the action.

> If I cannot solve it safely, I would escalate it.

## Linux level
> My professional command-line experience has mainly been in Windows.

> I have recently started learning Linux and Bash for infrastructure work.

> I understand the basic concepts and commands, and I am continuing to build this skill.

## Hardware level
> I do not have professional server hardware experience yet.

> I understand the basic server components and the diagnostic process.

> I am ready to learn the practical hardware procedures.

## Principle
Do not try to sound more experienced than you are. A safe, honest L1 answer is stronger than an invented L2 answer.
