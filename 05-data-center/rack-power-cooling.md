# Rack, Power and Cooling

## PSU vs PDU
**PSU:** inside the server; provides power to server components.

**PDU:** rack-level power distribution; supplies equipment and may provide monitoring/switching.

**UPS:** helps provide continuous power during certain power events.

## Cooling path
`Air enters → fans move air through chassis → components produce heat → hot air exits`

Check fan RPM, temperature sensors, airflow, blocked vents and physical obstructions.

## Interview answer
> Servers produce a lot of heat, so controlled airflow and working fans are necessary to keep hardware within safe temperatures.
